mShiny+

Privacy Policy — last updated July 2026

mShiny+ does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the app does with your data

mShiny+ has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, no user accounts, and makes no network requests of any kind. It has no way to send anything off your device.

ROMs and save files

Any ROM or save file you import stays on your device, in the app's own storage, visible in the Files app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data with it.
