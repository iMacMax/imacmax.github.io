mShiny+

Support

mShiny+ is a Game Boy Advance emulator for iOS and iPadOS that runs multiple games at once from a single controller.

Getting help

If something isn't working, or you have a question or feature request, reach out at imacmax@proton.me and I'll get back to you.

Games

mShiny+ does not include, supply, or link to any games. You provide your own ROM files, from games you legally own.
